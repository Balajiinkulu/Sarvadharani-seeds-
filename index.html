Raw Purchase Entry

ⓘ A focused purchase entry for raw seed. It posts as a normal Purchase voucher (increases stock, updates the vendor's ledger, applies GST as usual) — just streamlined for buying raw material. Choose whichever unit this purchase was actually measured in (Quintal or Bags) — the quantity is recorded as entered, with no automatic conversion between the two, so keep it consistent per item where it matters to you.

Trial Balance

ⓘ Every party and Cash/Bank/Capital account's closing balance, as of today, plus Sales, Purchase, and net GST (Duties & Taxes) accumulated all-time. Total Dr should equal Total Cr — if not, it's usually a sign an Opening Balance was entered inconsistently somewhere. This does not include the value of stock on hand as a ledger figure, since stock is tracked separately in the Stock reports.

Start New Financial Year
ⓘ This takes every party's and account's CURRENT closing balance (shown above) and sets it as their new Opening Balance, dated from the day you choose below. It does not delete, hide, or lock any past voucher — every past entry stays fully visible, editable, and deletable exactly as before. It only changes where each ledger's running balance "starts counting from" going forward. You can always undo this manually afterwards by editing a party/account's Opening Balance and Opening Balance As Of date back in Alter Party / Accounts.
